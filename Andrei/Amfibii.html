2 SPECIE

Axolotl

The axolotl, or Mexican walking fish, is an aquatic salamander famous for its regenerative abilities. It can regenerate limbs, the spinal cord, and even parts of its heart and brain.

Cane Toad

The cane toad is a large, invasive species known for its toxic skin secretions. Originally introduced to Australia for pest control, it has become a significant environmental threat.

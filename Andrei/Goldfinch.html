European Goldfinch

Population Size

Common across Europe

Life Span

3-5 years

Top Speed

40 km/h (25 mph)

Weight

18-22 grams (0.6-0.8 oz)

Wingspan

21-25 cm (8.3-9.8 inches)

Description

The European Goldfinch (*Carduelis carduelis*) is a small passerine bird known for its vibrant plumage, with a red face and yellow wing patches. It is found across Europe and parts of North Africa, and it is often seen in gardens, fields, and open woodlands. The European Goldfinch is a social bird and feeds mainly on seeds, especially thistles. It is also known for its beautiful song. Though common, its population has declined in some areas due to habitat loss, but it remains widespread and abundant.

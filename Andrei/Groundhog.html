Description

The groundhog, also known as a woodchuck or whistle-pig, is a rodent species belonging to the squirrel family. It is found primarily in North America and is known for its burrowing habits. Groundhogs are well-known for predicting the arrival of spring during the famous Groundhog Day tradition. Their burrows provide shelter and protection from predators, and they are often seen in fields and woodlands.

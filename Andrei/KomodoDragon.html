Komodo Dragon

Population Size

Estimated 5,000 individuals

Life Span

20-30 years

Top Speed

20 km/h (12 mph)

Weight

70-90 kg (154-198 lbs)

Length

2-3 meters (6.5-10 feet)

Description

The Komodo Dragon (*Varanus komodoensis*) is a large species of lizard found primarily on the islands of Komodo, Rinca, Flores, and Gili Motang in Indonesia. Known for its size, it can reach lengths of up to 3 meters (10 feet) and weighs up to 90 kg (198 lbs). It is a carnivore and has a venomous bite, using a combination of bacteria and venom to subdue prey. Komodo Dragons are apex predators in their ecosystem and play an important role in maintaining the health of their environment. They are listed as vulnerable due to habitat loss and human activities.

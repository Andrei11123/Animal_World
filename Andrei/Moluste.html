2 SPECIE

Gigant Squid

The giant squid is a deep-ocean cephalopod known for its massive size and elusive nature. It can grow up to 43 feet long and has the largest eyes in the animal kingdom.

Colossal Squid

The colossal squid is an even larger, more aggressive relative of the giant squid. It lives in the frigid waters of the Southern Ocean and can grow up to 46 feet long.

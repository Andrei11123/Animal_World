Ocean Sunfish

Population Size

Unknown

Life Span

Up to 10 years in the wild

Top Speed

3.2 km/h (2 mph)

Weight

Up to 2,300 kg (5,070 lbs)

Length

Up to 3.3 meters (10.8 feet)

Description

The Ocean Sunfish (*Mola mola*) is one of the heaviest known bony fishes in the world, reaching weights of up to 2,300 kg (5,070 lbs). Native to tropical and temperate waters worldwide, these unique fish are recognizable by their flattened, disk-like body and their tendency to bask at the surface of the water. They feed primarily on jellyfish but also consume small fish, algae, and plankton. Despite their large size, ocean sunfish are harmless to humans. However, they are vulnerable due to threats like accidental bycatch in fishing gear and plastic pollution.

Reptili

2 SPECIE

Komodo Dragon

The Komodo Dragon is the largest living lizard, found on a few Indonesian islands. Known for its powerful bite and toxic saliva, it is a top predator in its ecosystem.

Black Mamba

The Black Mamba is one of the fastest and deadliest snakes in the world, found in Africa. It can move at speeds up to 12 mph and delivers a potent venom.

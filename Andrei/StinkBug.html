Brown Marmorated Stink Bug

Population Size

Widespread and growing

Life Span

6-8 months

Top Speed

Not applicable

Weight

0.02-0.05 grams

Length

12-17 mm (0.5-0.7 inches)

Description

The Brown Marmorated Stink Bug (*Halyomorpha halys*) is an invasive species native to East Asia but has spread to many parts of the world, including Europe and North America. Recognizable by its shield-shaped body and mottled brown coloration, this bug is infamous for its unpleasant odor when threatened or crushed. It is a significant agricultural pest, feeding on a wide variety of crops such as fruits, vegetables, and grains. The Brown Marmorated Stink Bug poses a threat to farmers and ecosystems due to its rapid reproduction and adaptability. Efforts are ongoing to control its population through natural predators and other methods.
